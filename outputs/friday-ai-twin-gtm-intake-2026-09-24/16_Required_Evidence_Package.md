## Evidence to collect for the strongest case

Use one real, permission-cleared project issue. Capture: (1) the original group promise and meeting-minute owner evidence; (2) the expected artifact and deadline; (3) the current check showing why Friday judged a risk; (4) Friday's inferred contributor, lead, resolution path, and acceptance standard; (5) approval decision if required; (6) the sent group reminder and lead notice with receipts; (7) a later reply, document, or task-status update; (8) the final evidence and close/open decision; and (9) timestamps for first detectable signal, outreach, reply, and verified resolution. Redact names and business content, but preserve source relationships.

## One usable quantitative comparison design

Measure a matched set of eligible risks handled manually and with Friday. Report median minutes to appropriate owner contact, human touches, correct-owner rate, false alert rate, and evidence-backed resolution rate, with sample size, observation dates, connector scope, source coverage, and reviewer. Do not infer “project completed because of Friday” from a reminder alone; document the counterfactual as carefully as possible.
