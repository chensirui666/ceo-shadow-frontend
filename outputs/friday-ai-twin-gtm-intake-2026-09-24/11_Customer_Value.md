# 11. Customer Value — Earlier Intervention, Less Chasing, Clearer Closure

## The observable change

The feature-owner-confirmed loop changes a project lead's job from **personally remembering every promise and chasing every missing deliverable** to **reviewing the exceptions, helping remove blockers, and making the decisions only that person can make**. Friday reads authorized work signals, finds a credible risk before the deadline is lost, contacts the relevant contributor and lead under its safety/privacy rule, and checks whether the work meets an inferred acceptance standard.

That is the value to test. It is **not** a claim that Friday guarantees the document will be delivered or that all projects will finish on time.

## The most important measures

1. **Time from first detectable risk to appropriate contact.** A short interval only matters if the contact is correct and useful.
2. **Manager touchpoints per resolved risk.** Count searches, checks, messages, approvals, and corrections performed by a person.
3. **Evidence-backed resolution rate.** Count issues closed with proof matching the acceptance standard, not just messages sent.
4. **False or harmful action rate.** Wrong-person contact, stale reminder, privacy breach, and premature closure must be visible beside success metrics.
5. **Downstream milestone outcome.** Did early intervention prevent an avoidable delay? This requires a matched baseline, not an anecdote alone.

All numeric outcomes above are **Not yet measured**. The source for the operating mechanism is the feature owner's 2026-09-24 description, not a benchmark.

## Business value hypotheses

A company might see fewer deadline surprises, faster unblock decisions, lower manager coordination load, and more predictable project delivery. These are **hypotheses**, not demonstrated ROI. The value is likely strongest where work commitments happen in conversation but the finished work lives elsewhere, and where the lead oversees several dependencies. If a team already has disciplined task entry, reliable owners, and automatic status checks, the incremental benefit may be smaller.

There is no evidence in the provided material for a sales-cycle, revenue, meeting-load, or operational-cost percentage. Do not use such figures in customer materials until a measured pilot ties them to this workflow.

## Short illustrative example

A project lead does not notice that the promised compliance document is still missing. Friday surfaces the gap from authorized work context, reminds the contributor, alerts the lead, and stays with the issue until a current document or other agreed evidence satisfies the acceptance standard. The lead spends attention on helping the contributor, not discovering the problem from scratch. **This is a feature-owner scenario, not a recorded customer outcome.**

**Public claim readiness:** It is reasonable to say the feature is designed and confirmed by the owner to perform proactive risk follow-through. “Saves X hours” or “improves completion by Y%” is unsupported.
