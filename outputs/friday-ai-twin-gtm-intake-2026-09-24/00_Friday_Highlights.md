# Friday AI Twin: Candidate Highlights and Selected Deep Dive

**Purpose:** Internal factual intake for GTM. **Date:** 2026-09-24. **Product focus:** proactive work execution.

**Customer reason to buy:** Important work should not depend on a manager remembering every promise. Friday catches a delivery risk while there is still time to act, brings in the people who can resolve it, and keeps checking until the outcome is evidenced. The business value to prove is fewer avoidable deadline surprises and less manager time spent chasing status; no improvement figure has been measured yet.

## Selection and fact boundary

The selected customer outcome is the **risk-to-resolution loop**, not Memory, summarization, or a task list. Memory and distillation are supporting inputs when available. A risk report alone does not prove that Friday contacted the owner or resolved the issue.

The reviewed Friday AI Twin Task PRD explicitly describes a narrower, unimplemented first-version Task page with manual completion and no owner assignment. The underlying CEO Agent work-tracking materials describe owner evidence, follow-up, reply handling, and completion verification, and the feature owner separately confirms that the full risk-to-resolution workflow is running. The older Task page PRD does not describe that running workflow. The 17 sections distinguish **owner-confirmed operation**, **documented mechanism**, **Friday page specification**, **illustrative example**, and **unmeasured outcome**.
